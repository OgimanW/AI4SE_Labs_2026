SUPPORT TEAM SURVEY - SUMMARY (May 2025)

Respondents: 9 support agents

Q1. Biggest pain points
 - 7 of 9: we cannot see a rider's ride history when they call us; we need it in the
   admin console to answer complaints.
 - 5 of 9: refunds take too long because we must email finance; support should be able
   to issue refunds directly in the admin console.

Q2. Other comments
 - The admin console should load a rider profile in under 2 seconds.
 - One agent asked for a better parking spot near the office.
