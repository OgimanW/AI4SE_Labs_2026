# Rider feature wishlist (collected from the community forum)

- Live map that shows where free bikes are parked right now (most requested!)
- Reserve a bike for up to 10 minutes before reaching it - would be nice
- Push notification when my reservation is about to expire
- Monthly subscription plan with unlimited 30-minute rides
- The app should predict where I want to go before I think of it, with 100% accuracy,
  and teleport the bike to me
- Dark mode, if possible
